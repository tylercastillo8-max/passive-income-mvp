# Kill criteria and continuation metrics

This project should stay a small revenue experiment. Do not build a backend, user accounts, or a paid pack until there is evidence of demand.

## First 7 days: measure

- Unique visitors from launch channels.
- Clicks on the free generator call to action.
- Number of generated kits or export button clicks if analytics can measure events.
- Waitlist signups after the form is connected.
- Qualitative replies asking for a specific template pack.

## 7-day kill signals

Consider pausing or changing the idea if all of these are true after real distribution:

- Fewer than 100 qualified visitors from at least 5 launch channels.
- Fewer than 5 waitlist signups after the form is connected.
- No comments or replies saying the tool solves a real onboarding problem.
- Users understand the tool but do not want templates, downloads, or a paid pack.

## 7-day continue signals

Keep iterating if any of these happen:

- 10 or more waitlist signups.
- 3 or more people ask for a specific service-provider template.
- 5% or more of visitors click the waitlist call to action.
- A newsletter, community, or curator shares the tool without being paid.

## 30-day kill signals

Kill or pivot the paid-pack idea if, after consistent non-spam distribution:

- Fewer than 300 qualified visitors arrive.
- Fewer than 25 people join the waitlist.
- No single service niche clearly stands out in feedback.
- Visitors use the free generator but ignore the template pack preview.
- Maintaining or promoting the page requires more effort than the value of the signal.

## 30-day build-the-paid-pack signals

Create the first paid template pack if at least two of these are true:

- 50 or more waitlist signups.
- 10 or more replies mention the same service niche or paid template need.
- Waitlist conversion is 5% or higher from qualified traffic.
- At least 3 people explicitly say they would pay $9 to $19 for the pack.
- A launch channel produces repeat traffic without paid ads.

## What to do if killed

Keep the free static tool live if it costs nothing, but stop active work. Reuse the generator code and launch docs for a narrower tool, such as a web design onboarding kit, SEO onboarding kit, or virtual assistant client onboarding kit.
