# Launch risk review

## Reviewed items

- Broken links: internal links now point to existing page anchors or the static `templates/` page. The app avoids fake external links and does not connect payments before an offer exists.
- Positioning: the homepage now names the exact audience: freelancers, consultants, agencies, and service providers.
- Pain clarity: the copy explains repeated kickoff emails, unclear expectations, missing assets, and late access collection.
- Result clarity: the primary promise is a client onboarding packet, timeline, access checklist, kickoff questions, and kickoff email in minutes.
- SEO basics: the app includes a stronger page title, meta description, Open Graph tags, keyword-focused sections, FAQ content, and internal anchor links.
- Mobile layout: the layout uses responsive grids and collapses the navigation, generator, pricing cards, waitlist, and template previews on small screens.
- Privacy and disclaimer language: the footer states that generated text stays in the browser and the waitlist form is a placeholder until connected to an email provider.
- Trust risks: no fake testimonials, no fake usage numbers, no income claims, no payment links, and no regulated advice claims were added.

## Remaining pre-launch risks

- The Open Graph preview has no custom image yet. This is acceptable for a first launch, but a simple branded image would improve link sharing.
- The waitlist form is intentionally not connected. The owner must connect it to an email provider before expecting signups to be stored.
- The template pack is only previewed. The project should not connect payments until waitlist or traffic signals justify creating the pack.
