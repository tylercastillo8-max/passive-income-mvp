Client onboarding FAQ

      
Questions freelancers ask before sending an onboarding packet.

      

        

          What is a client onboarding packet?
          
It is a short document that tells a new client what happens next, what assets or logins are needed, how feedback works, and who owns each step.

        

        

          Who is WelcomeKit for?
          
It is for freelancers, consultants, agencies, and service providers who sell project-based or retainer services and want cleaner client starts.

        

        

          Does WelcomeKit store client information?
          
No. The generator runs in the browser and does not send form data to a backend.

        

        

          Can I use this with real clients?
          
Yes, but review and edit the generated text so it matches your process, tone, scope, and client relationship.

        

        

          Is this a legal contract?
          
No. WelcomeKit creates operational templates only. It is not legal, financial, tax, medical, or professional advice.

        

        

          What will be in the paid template pack?
          
The planned pack includes niche onboarding kits, client reminder emails, access request templates, feedback scripts, SOPs, and client portal copy.
