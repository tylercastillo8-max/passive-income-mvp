# Monetization plan

## Positioning

WelcomeKit should stay useful for free. The free generator creates trust and attracts people who already have a business problem: they need to onboard a new client professionally.

## Primary offer: premium template pack

Create a downloadable pack priced around $9 to $19 only after the free tool is live and waitlist/feedback signals justify the work. Keep it lightweight and low-support.

Suggested contents:

- 10 polished kickoff email variants.
- 6 client access request templates.
- 5 client feedback reminder emails.
- 3 project delay or blocker update templates.
- A Google Docs onboarding packet template.
- A Notion or Markdown client portal outline.
- A one-page freelancer onboarding SOP.

Manual action required: first connect the waitlist and validate demand. If the kill criteria justify continuing, create the paid product on a no-monthly-fee platform and paste the checkout link into the pricing section of `index.html`.

## Secondary offer: transparent affiliates

Only add affiliate links that are relevant and genuinely useful, such as scheduling, forms, project management, or documentation tools with free tiers. Label every affiliate link clearly. Do not imply the user must buy anything to use WelcomeKit.

## Sponsor path

If the page receives steady traffic, sell one simple sponsorship placement to a relevant tool. Keep it manually renewed monthly or quarterly so it does not require engineering.

## What not to monetize

- Do not sell legal contract language.
- Do not make income claims.
- Do not collect sensitive client information.
- Do not require a subscription until there is evidence users repeatedly need saved kits.
